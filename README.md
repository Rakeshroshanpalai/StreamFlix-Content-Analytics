# StreamFlix Content Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding StreamFlix subscriber engagement, content performance, user experience, and catalogue investment.

The project covers data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, KPI calculation, Power BI dashboard development, and management reporting.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze StreamFlix data and provide business insights that can help management understand:

- Subscriber engagement and viewing behavior
- Content performance by genre and title
- Subscriber demographics and plans
- User experience across devices
- Ratings and review sentiment
- Originals versus licensed content
- Content investment efficiency
- Upcoming licence expiries
- Key business KPIs

## Key Findings

- Total watch hours reached approximately 3.33 million hours.
- The active subscriber rate is 74.66%.
- The churn rate is 25.34%.
- Average content completion rate is 65.31%.
- Licensed content represents 78.16% of the catalogue.
- Originals represent 21.84% of the catalogue.
- Animation has the highest watch hours per $1K of licensing spend in the analysis.
- Smart TV is the leading viewing device by viewing sessions.
- Positive reviews represent the largest share of review sentiment.
- May 2026 recorded the highest monthly watch-hours level in the available data.
